About

I make personal finance easier to understand for students and early-career professionals. As an MBA student and working professional in background verification, I share practical explainers on budgeting, credit cards and credit scores, SIPs and long-term investing, inflation, and financial decisions that affect everyday life.

My approach is simple: explain the concept, show the real-world impact, and encourage readers to verify facts and make informed decisions, not chase quick returns. I’m also learning how AI and data are shaping finance, and sharing that journey.
